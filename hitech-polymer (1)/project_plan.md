# High-Tech Polymers

## 1. Project Description
A premium B2B industrial corporate website for High-Tech Polymers, a leading manufacturer specializing in Azodicarbonamide (ADC) blowing agents and EVA Processing Technology since 2007. The website targets manufacturers and industrial clients seeking specialized chemical solutions, providing comprehensive information about products, manufacturing processes, quality standards, and industry applications. Core value: showcasing technical expertise, building credibility, and generating business inquiries.

## 2. Page Structure
- `/` - Home (hero slideshow, about, why choose us, products, industries, manufacturing process, quality, gallery, values, FAQ, contact form)
- `/about` - About Us (company history, mission, team, manufacturing)
- `/products` - All Products (ADC blowing agents, EVA processing products)
- `/products/:id` - Product Detail (specs, applications, technical data)
- `/industries` - Industries Served (footwear, automotive, construction, packaging, etc.)
- `/quality` - Quality Standards (certifications, testing, R&D)
- `/contact` - Contact & Inquiry
